Amaze Daily Challenge - Account Deletion

Amaze Daily Challenge is published by Apps Studio Global. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us at appsstudioglobal@gmail.com .

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to appsstudioglobal@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Amaze Daily Challenge Account Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us that you want your Amaze Daily Challenge account deleted.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership.

What data will be deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your Firebase account and associated cloud game-progress data, such as saved XP, coins, streaks, scores, and daily challenge progress, where applicable.

Data retention

We will process verified deletion requests within 30 days. Data may be retained longer only where required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

Guest users

Guest progress may be stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored progress.
